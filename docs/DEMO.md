# Demonstration and presentation guide

## Six-minute demonstration

| Time | Presenter | Action / point |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:45 | Person 2 | Describe a student who can repeat a definition but struggles to explain a trade-off. Show the index concept. |
| 0:45–2:00 | Invi | Enter a short explanation, answer the defense question, show the learning activity and new transfer scenario. State whether feedback is live AI or an offline demo. |
| 2:00–2:45 | Person 3 | Show one anonymized answer record and the kinds of test cases used. Explain why source labels matter. |
| 2:45–4:00 | Person 4 | Explain independent grading and the three-method comparison. Show real results only if the study is complete; otherwise show the protocol and label it planned. |
| 4:00–5:00 | Invi | Explain one observed failure case and why a score is not a verdict on a student’s ability. |
| 5:00–6:00 | All | Each states their contribution, one lesson learned, and one remaining limitation. |

For juniors, invite them to explain why “an index makes queries faster” is incomplete. Let the follow-up reveal the trade-off. Do not embarrass or rank volunteers. For faculty, be ready to explain condition isolation, rubric design, reviewer qualifications, data leakage, missing data and the primary metric.

The student takeaway is a reusable habit: explain a mechanism, test it with a counterexample, repair a gap, then try a different situation. Whether this prototype improves learning is a question for a controlled study.

## Questions to prepare for

- Why is this more than a chatbot? Point to the structured loop, explicit rubric and transparent evaluation protocol; do not claim these mechanisms are unique without literature support.
- Why trust the score? It is provisional feedback; human agreement and failure cases must be measured.
- What is original? Describe the tested configuration, local context and evidence contribution after reviewing related work.
- Why two human reviewers? To expose disagreement rather than treating a single opinion as perfect ground truth.
- What if Method 3 loses? Report it. That is still a useful result.
- Have you shown learning gains? Only say yes after an appropriate participant study; the current workflow alone does not establish them.
